Our Elixirs

Green Tea Cooler

Green Tea Cooler is a refreshing beverage made from brewed green tea, lightly sweetened with honey, and infused with a splash of lemon juice for a zesty twist. It's perfect for a relaxing afternoon, offering both hydration and the antioxidant benefits of green tea.

Raspberry Ice Concentration

Raspberry Ice Concentration is a fruity and tangy drink blending fresh raspberry puree with crushed ice and a touch of mint, delivering a burst of berry flavor with a cooling sensation, ideal for summer days.

Power Punch

Punch is an energizing blend of mixed berries, orange juice, and a hint of ginger, packed with vitamins and natural sugars to give you a revitalizing boost, making it great for pre-workout hydration or a midday pick-me-up. Each drink offers a unique flavor profile, catering to different moods and occasions.
