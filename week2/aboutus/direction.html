Directions

To travel from BCIT's Burnaby Campus to the Downtown Campus at 555 Seymour Street, Vancouver, you have several transportation options: By Car: Route: From the Burnaby Campus at 3700 Willingdon Avenue, head south on Willingdon Avenue. Merge onto the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1) westbound. Take Exit 26 for Hastings Street and continue west. Turn left onto Seymour Street; the campus will be on your right. Parking: Downtown Vancouver has various public parking facilities near the campus. Be prepared for traffic, especially during peak hours, and consider parking availability and costs. By Public Transit: Route: From the Burnaby Campus, walk to the nearest bus stop and take a bus to a SkyTrain station on the Expo Line. Board the SkyTrain towards Waterfront Station and alight at Granville Station. The Downtown Campus is a short walk from there. Planning: Use TransLink's Trip Planner to find the most efficient route and schedule.
