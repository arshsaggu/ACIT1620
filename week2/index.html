Welcome to the CIT Lounge

It is a warm and inviting space designed for students in the Computer Information Technology (CIT) program to relax, collaborate, and connect. The lounge offers a comfortable environment with seating areas, workspaces, and resources for both socializing and studying. It serves as a hub for teamwork, informal meetings, and sharing ideas, fostering a sense of community among CIT students. Whether you're unwinding between classes or brainstorming a project, the CIT Lounge is the perfect place to engage with peers and build lasting connections
