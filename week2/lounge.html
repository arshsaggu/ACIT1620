Welcome to the CIT Lounge


The CIT Lounge is a vibrant, cozy spot designed for relaxation, socializing, and productivity. Nestled in the heart of the community, it offers a welcoming atmosphere where guests can unwind with premium drinks and light refreshments. The lounge takes pride in its signature elixirs crafted to provide a perfect balance of taste and wellness benefits, making it a go-to destination for both casual visits and focused work sessions. Whether you're stopping by for a break or meeting friends, the CIT Lounge promises a refreshing experience. Among the lounge's standout offerings is the Green Tea Cooler, a light and refreshing beverage that combines brewed green tea, honey, and a splash of lemon juice for a gentle citrus twist. This drink is perfect for those seeking a calm, health-conscious option with antioxidants and a smooth finish. It's a top choice for guests looking to stay energized while working on projects or enjoying a quiet afternoon.
